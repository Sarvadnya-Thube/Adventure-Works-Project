# Adventure-Works-Project
## Project Objective
This project's goal is to use Excel, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI to turn raw data into useful insights for analyzing sales performance and business trends. Finding key performance indicators (KPIs), comprehending consumer and product behavior, assessing regional performance, and monitoring time-based sales trends ..
## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Sarvadnya-Thube/Adventure-Works-Project/blob/main/AdventureWorks%20-%20updated%20.zip">Dataset</a>
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
    Products Purchased per Order :- 2
    Average Order Value (AOV) :- 1.06K
    Total Sales Revenue :- $29.4M
    Production Cost :- $17.3M
    Total Customers :- 18K
    Total Profit :- 12.08M
    Tax Amount :- $2.3M
    Total Orders :- 28K
## Insights 
    Sales peaked in 2013, then declined in 2014.
    North America & USA lead total sales.
    Bikes category dominates overall revenue.
    Age group 50–75 contributes highest sales.
    Weekday sales exceed weekend sales.
    Male & female sales are nearly equal.
